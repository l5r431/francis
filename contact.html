Request a visit

What happens on a visit

  1. A warm welcome

    You and your child are greeted at the gate and shown around the campus.

  2. See learning happen

    Visit the library, computer lab and science labs, and watch a lesson in progress.

  3. Meet the Head

    Ask anything over a cup of tea, and meet the boarding warden if you are considering boarding.

  4. Take your time

    Leave with a prospectus and no pressure. Come back as often as you like.

Getting here

  • Boda boda and taxi

    Regular boda bodas and taxis serve the roads around Namagoma. Ask the driver for St. Francis High School.

  • School bus

    School bus routes cover several Kampala suburbs. Seats are booked termly.

  • By car

    Parking and drop-off bays are at the main gate from 7:00. Please do not block the access road.

Common questions

Can my child spend a morning in class before applying?

Yes. Shadow mornings run most Wednesdays. Tell us the class and we will pair your child with a student guide.

How soon will I hear back?

We reply to visit requests within two working days.

Can siblings visit too?

Of course. Please mention their classes so we can plan.

Can my child board?

Yes. We offer day and boarding places in every class. Book a boarding tour to see the dormitories and meet the warden.

Do you cater for allergies?

Yes. Our catering team follows each student's plan, and the kitchen is nut aware.

Where can I find policies?

Policies are shared with families on registration and in the parent portal.
