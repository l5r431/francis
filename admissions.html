Joining St. Francis

Visit us, apply, sit a short assessment and receive an offer.

  1. Visit us
  2. Register
  3. Assessment
  4. Offer

How to apply

  1. Visit us

    Come to an open day or book a private tour with your child.

  2. Register

    Fill in the form online. S.1 entry needs PLE results and S.5 entry needs UCE results.

  3. Assessment

    A short test and a friendly interview with a teacher. We want to know how you think.

  4. Offer

    We reply within two weeks and agree day or boarding with you.

Before your first day

Tick things off as you go. Your progress is saved on this device.

Day scholar or boarder

  • Day scholars

    Arrive by 7:15, join assembly and go home after games at 17:00. School bus routes are available.

  • Boarders

    Dormitories with wardens and matrons, supervised evening prep and weekend visiting days.

  • Orientation week

    New S.1 and S.5 students start with tours, library and computer lab training and meeting their house.

Key dates

Open days and reporting

Whole-school open day: Saturday 14 November 2026. S.1 reporting and orientation day: Wednesday 3 February 2027.

Registration deadlines

S.1 and S.5 entry for 2027: 15 January 2027. Later registrations are welcome if places remain.

Assessment days

Entrance assessments run in December and January for registered applicants.

Offers and acceptance

Offers go out within two weeks of the assessment. Families have two weeks to accept and pay the admission fee.
